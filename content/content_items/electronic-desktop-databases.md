      <h5 style="margin-top: 0;">Key Info</h5>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">Each row in a database table represents a single
        <strong>record</strong> (one employee), and each column represents a
        <strong>field</strong> (one type of information). The Employee ID field
        ensures every record can be uniquely identified, which is essential for
        accurate data retrieval and management.</p>
    </div>
    <div
      style="border-left: 4px solid #C755FF; background: #f9fafb; padding: 12px 16px; margin: 16px 0;">
      <h5 style="margin-top: 0;">Why This Matters</h5>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">Electronic desktop databases allow businesses to
        store and retrieve large amounts of structured information quickly and
        accurately. Whether managing employee records, tracking finances, or
        analysing customer behaviour, databases are essential tools that improve
        efficiency, support decision-making, and ensure that business-critical data
        is always organised and accessible.</p>
    </div>

    <h5
      style="text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 0.8rem; letter-spacing: 0.05em; color: #6b7280; margin-bottom: 8px;">
      KEY TAKEAWAYS</h5>
    <p>Electronic desktop databases are software applications used to organise,
      store, retrieve, and manage structured data on a local computer. They are
      widely used in business for human resources management, financial planning,
      and customer relationship management. A database is made up of records (rows)
      and fields (columns), with each record representing a unique entry — such as
      an individual employee — and each field capturing a specific type of
      information about that entry.</p>
